# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when mistakes happen.
- Keep security reports and private repository material confidential.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is harmful.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
